## 3D face model

`public/models/face.glb` is converted from **ICT-FaceKit** (Light version), with its eyes and lash cards kept, a subset of its expression shapes, and compression applied. Hair, lashes, skin, makeup and guides are drawn by this site's code. It is the demo's fallback face and is not shown while photographic face packs are listed.
